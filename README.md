## Overview

Course material is usually scattered across chat groups, drives and screenshots. **Dof3tna** gathers everything a student needs for the semester into one fast, offline-friendly app, and keeps it up to date every day.

Pick your year and specialization once, and the app shows only what is relevant to you: lectures, summaries, timetables, announcements and more.

## Installation guide

### Android

1. **Download** the latest [`dof3tna.apk`](https://github.com/OsamaMabrouk0/dof3tna/releases/latest/download/dof3tna.apk) on your phone.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow **"Install from this source"** for your browser or file manager.
3. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect shows a warning for an app from outside the store, tap **Install anyway**.
4. Open **Dof3tna**, choose your **year** and **specialization**, and you're ready to go.
5. When asked, allow **notifications** so you never miss new files or announcements.

> The app is distributed directly as an APK so you receive updates faster, without waiting for store reviews.

### iPhone / iPad

1. Open [**dof3tna.great-site.net**](https://dof3tna.great-site.net) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

The web app then launches from your Home Screen like a regular app.

## FAQ & troubleshooting

<details>
<summary><b>Is the app free?</b></summary>

Yes. Downloading and using Dof3tna is completely free.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why isn't it on Google Play?</b></summary>

Direct distribution lets updates reach you immediately, without waiting for store review.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The install is blocked or I see a Play Protect warning.</b></summary>

This is normal for any app installed from outside a store. Allow installation from your browser or file manager when prompted, then tap **Install anyway**.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>"App not installed" appears when updating.</b></summary>

Make sure you downloaded the file from this repository's official [Releases](https://github.com/OsamaMabrouk0/dof3tna/releases) page. If the problem continues, uninstall the old version and install the new one again.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>I don't receive notifications.</b></summary>

Open your phone's **Settings → Apps → Dof3tna → Notifications** and make sure they are enabled. Also check that battery optimization isn't restricting the app.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there an iPhone version?</b></summary>

Yes, as a web app. Follow the [iPhone installation steps](#iphone--ipad) above.
</details>
