## Why Dof3tna?

Course material is usually scattered across chat groups, drives and screenshots. Dof3tna gathers everything a student needs for the semester into a single, fast, offline-friendly app — and keeps it up to date every day.

## Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔄 **Always up to date** | Lectures and summaries are refreshed daily instead of being stuck on an old semester. |
| 📴 **Study offline** | Download lectures inside the app and open them anywhere, without internet. |
| 🔔 **Smart notifications** | Get notified when new files, announcements or reminders are published for your year. |
| 🎯 **Personalised content** | Pick your year and specialization once; everything is filtered for you automatically. |
| 🗓️ **Schedules** | Lecture and section timetables for your group, plus "today's lectures" on the home screen. |
| 🧮 **GPA & grade calculator** | See your grade, its percentage and how far you are from the next level. |
| 🏛️ **Buildings & rooms** | Search for any hall or building on campus. |
| 🔗 **Batch links** | Group chats and shared drives in one place. |
| 🌗 **Light / Dark / Auto** | A full RTL interface with a theme that follows your device. |
| 🧹 **Storage control** | Clear the cache without touching your downloaded files. |

## Download & install

### Android

1. Download the latest **[dof3tna.apk](../../releases/latest/download/dof3tna.apk)**.
2. If your phone asks, allow **"Install from this source"** for your browser.
3. Open the file, tap **Install**, then choose your year and specialization.

> The app is distributed directly as an APK so you get updates faster, without waiting for store reviews.

### iPhone & other devices

Open the **[web version](https://dof3tna.great-site.net)** in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it behaves like a regular app.

## Updates

New versions are published on the **[Releases](../../releases)** page. To update, install the newest APK over the existing app — your data and downloads are kept.

## Built with

- **Flutter / Dart** — one codebase, smooth RTL interface
- **Supabase** — content, schedules and realtime updates
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notifications
